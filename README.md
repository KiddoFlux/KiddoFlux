# KiddoFlux

Mobile app that organizes the school run for parents. Neighbouring families from the same class or school share trips through a fair carpool rotation: each parent drives 3 to 5 of the 10 weekly slots (5 days × morning and afternoon), and drivers in a group differ by at most one ride per week. Every handover is confirmed in one tap (QR or PIN), only authorised adults can collect a child, a missing-child alert fires 2 minutes after the deadline, and child seats are matched for children under 135 cm.

## Business model

| Phase | Customer | Who pays | Price | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. B2B | Private primary and lower-secondary schools | The school | €1,500 per school per year (Standard), €3,500 per campus | Free pilot with 3 to 5 schools (Sibiu, Cluj), Jan–Mar 2027 |
| 2. B2G | Municipalities, for public schools | The city hall | About €1,000 per school per year, city dashboard included (hypothesis) | Starts after private-school traction, not before Q4 2027 |

Parents never pay, in either phase.

- Phase 1 scope: carpool rota with confirmed handovers.
- Phase 2 additions: city dashboard (cars removed, CO2 avoided) and a one-page report for residents.
